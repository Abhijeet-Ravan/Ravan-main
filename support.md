## What We Can Help With

Contact support for help with:

- account access;
- login, password, or authentication issues;
- organization and user management;
- Agni AI agent setup;
- inbound or outbound calling issues;
- phone number setup or routing;
- campaign and contact management;
- call recordings, transcripts, summaries, or call outcomes;
- CRM, calendar, scheduling, or webhook integrations;
- Zoom app installation, permissions, usage, or removal;
- billing, credits, subscriptions, or invoices;
- data deletion, export, or privacy requests;
- bug reports or service interruptions.

---

## How to Contact Support

Email **support@ravan.ai** with:

- your name;
- your work email;
- company or organization name;
- the product or integration involved, such as Agni, Zoom, CRM, calendar, phone numbers, or billing;
- a short description of the issue;
- screenshots, error messages, timestamps, call session IDs, meeting IDs, or affected contact/campaign names where relevant.

Please do not send passwords, full payment card numbers, private keys, or highly sensitive personal information in support emails.

---

## Expected Response Times

We aim to respond as follows:

| Request type | Target response |
|---|---:|
| Account access, billing, and general support | 1-2 business days |
| Production-impacting product issues | 1 business day |
| Security reports | As soon as reasonably possible |
| Privacy or data deletion requests | Within the time required by applicable law |

Response times may vary by plan, severity, region, holidays, and support volume.

---

## Zoom App Support

For Zoom app support, include:

- the Zoom account email used to install the app;
- your Agni organization/workspace name;
- the approximate time the issue occurred;
- the feature involved;
- any Zoom error message shown;
- whether you are trying to install, use, reconnect, disconnect, or remove the app.

Users can revoke the Zoom app from their Zoom account settings. If you also want us to delete related data retained in Agni, contact **privacy@ravan.ai** or **support@ravan.ai**.

---

## Privacy and Data Requests

For privacy rights requests, data deletion requests, or questions about how Agni processes personal information, contact **privacy@ravan.ai**.

If your information was processed by Agni on behalf of one of our business customers, we may direct your request to that customer or work with that customer to respond.

---

## Security Reports

If you believe you found a security vulnerability or unauthorized access issue, contact **security@ravan.ai**.

Please include enough detail for us to investigate. Do not publicly disclose the issue or access, modify, delete, or download data that does not belong to you.
