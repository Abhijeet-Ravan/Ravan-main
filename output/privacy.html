This Privacy Policy explains how Ravan AI LLC ("Ravan," "ravan.ai," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects personal information in connection with our websites, applications, products, integrations, and services, including Agni by ravan.ai ("Agni").

Agni is a business-facing AI calling platform that helps organizations create AI voice agents, manage calls and campaigns, connect phone numbers and integrations, book appointments, and analyze call outcomes.

1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies when you:

  • visit our websites;
  • create or use a ravan.ai or Agni account;
  • use Agni dashboards, APIs, voice agents, call tools, campaigns, integrations, or analytics;
  • connect or use a ravan.ai/Agni app or integration, including the Zoom integration;
  • communicate with us for support, sales, billing, or legal requests;
  • interact with an AI agent, call, message, meeting workflow, or other service powered by Agni.

This policy does not replace any agreement between Ravan and a business customer. Business customers may have additional contractual terms, data processing terms, or order forms that apply to their use of Agni.

2. Roles: Customers, End Users, and Call Participants

Agni is primarily provided to businesses and organizations. In many cases:

  • the business customer decides what data is uploaded, connected, called, recorded, analyzed, or synced through Agni;
  • Ravan processes that data to provide Agni to the business customer;
  • individuals who receive calls, join meetings, appear in contact lists, or otherwise interact with Agni are the customer's end users, contacts, leads, prospects, customers, team members, or other call participants.

Where a business customer determines why and how personal information is processed, that customer is responsible for providing required notices, obtaining required consents, maintaining lawful contact lists, honoring do-not-call obligations, and complying with applicable call recording, telemarketing, messaging, privacy, employment, health, financial, and consumer protection laws.

Ravan may also process certain information as an independent controller, including account, billing, security, website, support, and business operations data.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account and Organization Data

We may collect information such as:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • phone number;
  • password or authentication information;
  • business name;
  • organization or workspace membership;
  • role, permissions, and user settings;
  • profile information;
  • login, session, and security activity;
  • API keys and related account identifiers.

3.2 Product Configuration Data

When customers configure Agni, we may process:

  • AI agent names, descriptions, prompts, scripts, instructions, and beginning messages;
  • selected AI models, speech models, voice settings, language/accent settings, temperature, and behavior settings;
  • call settings, voicemail settings, silence handling, ring duration, maximum call duration, IVR options, and transfer settings;
  • agent tools, custom functions, webhook settings, schemas, and workflow configuration;
  • widget settings, consent text, custom form fields, branding, themes, and public booking or interaction pages;
  • knowledge-base settings, selected knowledge sources, exclusions, and retrieval configuration.

3.3 Contact, Campaign, and CRM Data

Customers may upload, import, create, sync, or manage information such as:

  • names;
  • phone numbers;
  • email addresses;
  • company or lead details;
  • tags, notes, status, segments, and custom fields;
  • campaign membership and campaign settings;
  • call schedules, reminders, follow-up status, and outcomes;
  • CRM records, appointment records, deal records, calendar records, or related integration data.

3.4 Call, Voice, and Communication Data

Depending on customer settings and the services used, Agni may process:

  • inbound call data;
  • outbound call data;
  • web call data;
  • caller and callee phone numbers;
  • caller name, email, or other identifying details where provided;
  • call status, timestamps, duration, channel, routing details, and disconnect reason;
  • call metadata and prompt dynamic variables provided by the customer;
  • live audio streams;
  • call recordings where enabled;
  • transcripts or conversation records where enabled;
  • summaries, sentiment, extracted fields, structured outputs, and post-call analysis;
  • latency, quality, usage, credit, and cost information;
  • error logs and diagnostic data needed to operate the service.

3.5 Zoom Integration Data

If you connect your Zoom account to Agni through Zoom OAuth, we may collect and process Zoom-related information only as needed to provide the Zoom-related features authorized by you or your organization. Depending on the integration configuration, permissions approved during authorization, and features used, this may include:

  • Zoom user profile information, such as name, email address, account ID, or user ID;
  • Zoom account or workspace identifiers;
  • meeting, calendar, or scheduling metadata needed to provide configured workflows;
  • meeting links, meeting IDs, topics, timestamps, host/participant metadata, or related records where authorized;
  • recordings, transcripts, summaries, or meeting content only if the integration feature requires it and the relevant permissions are granted;
  • authorization, token refresh, connection status, and revocation information for the Zoom integration.

We use Zoom data only to provide, maintain, secure, and support the Zoom-related features of Agni. We do not sell Zoom data. We do not use Zoom data to train models for other customers without express authorization.

Users and administrators can revoke Zoom access through their Zoom account settings and, where available, through the Agni dashboard. After revocation, Agni will stop accessing new Zoom data, although previously processed data may remain as described in this policy, customer settings, applicable agreements, and backup retention practices.

Customers may upload or connect business content to help agents answer accurately. This may include:

Agni may process this content to index, retrieve, summarize, and use it during agent interactions and testing.

Payment card or bank information may be processed directly by third-party payment processors. We do not intentionally store full payment card numbers unless expressly stated in a separate payment flow.

  • referring URLs;

  • cookie and similar technology identifiers;
  • product usage events;

3.9 Support, Sales, and Communications

When you contact us, we may collect:

  • company information;
  • messages, attachments, and support requests;
  • call, meeting, or demo notes;
  • feedback and survey responses.

5. AI Processing and Model Training

Agni uses artificial intelligence and related technologies to power voice agents, transcription, summarization, post-call analysis, knowledge retrieval, and workflow automation.

Customer content may be processed by AI systems and service providers to provide requested features. Customer content may include prompts, scripts, contact data, call metadata, audio, recordings, transcripts, summaries, extracted fields, Zoom data, CRM data, calendar data, and knowledge-base content.

Unless a customer expressly authorizes otherwise in writing, Ravan does not use customer call recordings, transcripts, Zoom data, contact lists, CRM data, or knowledge-base content to train models for other customers. We may use aggregated or de-identified operational information to maintain, secure, debug, and improve the service, provided it does not identify a customer or individual.

Third-party AI, speech, transcription, telephony, hosting, or infrastructure providers may process data on our behalf to provide the service. Their use of data is governed by our agreements with them and their applicable terms.
