This documentation explains how to connect, use, disconnect, and request deletion for the Agni by ravan.ai Zoom integration.

Agni is a product of Ravan AI LLC. Agni helps businesses use AI voice agents, calls, campaigns, appointments, integrations, and call intelligence workflows.

Important note: Users do not need to download or install a separate Agni application on their computer or phone. The Zoom connection happens through Zoom OAuth authorization. Zoom may refer to this process as adding or installing an app, but in Agni this simply means authorizing Agni to access the Zoom data permitted by the approved Zoom scopes.

1. What the Zoom Integration Does

The Agni Zoom integration allows authorized Agni users and organizations to connect a Zoom account to an Agni workspace through Zoom OAuth.

After a Zoom account is connected, Agni may use authorized Zoom API access to support Zoom-related workflows inside Agni. Depending on the app configuration, workspace settings, and permissions approved during OAuth authorization, Agni may help users:

  • connect a Zoom account to an Agni workspace;
  • identify the connected Zoom user or account;
  • fetch Zoom meeting or appointment metadata needed for configured Agni workflows;
  • create, display, sync, or send Zoom meeting links where a workflow requires it;
  • associate Zoom meetings or links with Agni contacts, appointments, campaigns, call sessions, or CRM workflows;
  • process meeting records, transcripts, recordings, or summaries only where the relevant feature is enabled and the required permission has been granted.

Agni only uses Zoom data to provide the Zoom-related functionality requested by the user or organization.

2. What This Integration Is Not

The Agni Zoom integration is not a separate desktop app, mobile app, browser extension, or Zoom client plugin that users need to download.

The connection works through OAuth:

  1. The user signs in to Agni.
  2. The user selects Connect Zoom.
  3. Agni redirects the user to Zoom.
  4. Zoom shows the requested permissions.
  5. The user authorizes the connection if they agree and have authority to do so.
  6. Zoom redirects the user back to Agni.
  7. Agni stores the authorization securely and uses it only for the approved Zoom-related workflows.

Zoom may still use marketplace terms such as add, install, remove, or deauthorize. In this documentation, connect means authorizing Zoom access through OAuth, and disconnect means removing or revoking that authorization.

3. Before You Connect Zoom

Before connecting Zoom to Agni, make sure:

  • you have an active Zoom account;
  • you have an Agni account or workspace;
  • your organization permits connecting Zoom to third-party applications;
  • you have the required Zoom permissions or administrator approval;
  • you understand the Zoom permissions requested during the OAuth authorization flow.

4. Connecting Your Zoom Account

  1. Sign in to your Agni account.
  2. Go to the Agni dashboard.
  3. Open Integrations or the relevant Zoom connection page.
  4. Select Connect Zoom.
  5. You will be redirected to Zoom for authorization.
  6. Review the permissions requested by the Agni Zoom integration.
  7. Authorize the connection if you agree and have authority to connect the Zoom account.
  8. After authorization, Zoom will redirect you back to Agni.
  9. Confirm that the Zoom connection shows as active in Agni.

If your organization requires administrator approval, a Zoom administrator may need to approve the integration before it can be connected or used.

5. Permissions and Data Access

The exact Zoom permissions requested are shown during the Zoom authorization flow. Agni only requests permissions needed to provide the configured Zoom-related functionality.

Depending on the app version and enabled features, Agni may access some or all of the following categories of Zoom data:

Data category Examples Purpose
Zoom user/account information User ID, account ID, name, email address Connect the correct Zoom user or account to Agni
Meeting metadata Meeting ID, topic, host, timestamps, meeting link Create, display, sync, or associate meetings with Agni workflows
Calendar or scheduling data Meeting time, duration, related scheduling information Coordinate appointment or meeting workflows
Meeting content, where enabled Recordings, transcripts, summaries, participant-related records Provide configured call intelligence or workflow features
Authorization data Access tokens, refresh tokens, connection status, revocation status Maintain, secure, refresh, or disconnect the integration

Agni does not access Zoom data that is not authorized by the permissions granted to the integration.

6. How Agni Uses Zoom Data

Agni may use Zoom data to:

  • connect a Zoom account to an Agni workspace;
  • enable Zoom-related workflows inside Agni;
  • sync or display meeting links and meeting metadata;
  • send Zoom meeting links through configured Agni workflows;
  • connect meetings or scheduled events to contacts, campaigns, appointments, or CRM records;
  • generate configured summaries, transcripts, or call intelligence outputs where enabled;
  • provide support, troubleshooting, security, and audit functions;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Agni does not sell Zoom data. Agni does not use Zoom data to train AI models for other customers unless expressly authorized in writing.

7. Using Zoom After It Is Connected

After Zoom is connected:

  1. Open Agni.
  2. Navigate to the workflow where Zoom is used, such as integrations, appointments, meetings, contacts, campaigns, or call/session workflows.
  3. Choose the relevant Zoom-connected account or meeting option.
  4. Configure the workflow in Agni.
  5. Save the configuration.
  6. Test the workflow before using it with customers, leads, call participants, or meeting participants.

The available actions may depend on your Agni plan, organization settings, Zoom permissions, and the features enabled for your workspace.

8. Disconnecting or Revoking Zoom Authorization

You can disconnect Zoom from Agni by:

  1. Signing in to Agni.
  2. Going to Integrations or the Zoom connection settings.
  3. Selecting the connected Zoom account.
  4. Choosing Disconnect, Remove, or the equivalent action.

You can also revoke Agni's access from Zoom:

  1. Sign in to your Zoom account.
  2. Open Zoom App Marketplace or your account's installed apps settings.
  3. Find the Agni or ravan.ai integration.
  4. Choose Remove, Uninstall, Revoke, or the equivalent action shown by Zoom.

After disconnection or revocation, Agni will stop accessing new Zoom data through the revoked authorization. Previously processed data may remain in Agni according to customer settings, applicable agreements, the Privacy Policy, and backup retention practices.

10. Troubleshooting

The Zoom connection does not complete

Check that:

  • you are signed in to the correct Zoom account;
  • your organization allows third-party app authorizations;
  • you have administrator approval if required;
  • you are signed in to the correct Agni workspace.

The integration disconnected

Try reconnecting Zoom from the Agni integrations page. If the issue continues, contact support with the time of the issue and any error message shown.

The wrong Zoom account is connected

Disconnect the current Zoom account, sign in to the correct Zoom account, and reconnect through Agni.

Meeting data is missing

Confirm that:

  • the relevant Zoom permissions were granted;
  • the meeting belongs to the connected Zoom account;
  • the feature is enabled in Agni;
  • your Agni workspace has access to the relevant workflow.
