1. What the Zoom App Does

The Agni Zoom app allows authorized users and organizations to connect Zoom with Agni so Zoom-related workflows can be used inside Agni.

Depending on the app configuration and permissions approved during installation, the app may help users:

  • connect a Zoom account to an Agni workspace;
  • use Zoom identity or account information to authenticate or configure a workflow;
  • access meeting or scheduling metadata needed for configured workflows;
  • create, display, or sync Zoom meeting links where a workflow requires it;
  • associate Zoom-related records with Agni contacts, appointments, campaigns, call sessions, or CRM workflows;
  • process meeting records, transcripts, recordings, or summaries only where the relevant feature is enabled and the required permission has been granted.

Agni only uses Zoom data to provide the Zoom-related functionality requested by the user or organization.

2. Before You Install

Before installing the app, make sure:

  • you have an active Zoom account;
  • you have an Agni account or workspace;
  • your organization permits connecting Zoom to third-party applications;
  • you have the required Zoom permissions or administrator approval;
  • you understand what Zoom permissions are requested during installation.

3. Installation Steps

  1. Sign in to your Agni account.
  2. Go to the Agni dashboard.
  3. Open Integrations or the relevant Zoom connection page.
  4. Select Connect Zoom.
  5. You will be redirected to Zoom for authorization.
  6. Review the permissions requested by the Agni Zoom app.
  7. Approve the app if you agree and have authority to connect it.
  8. After approval, Zoom will redirect you back to Agni.
  9. Confirm that the Zoom connection shows as active in Agni.

If your organization requires administrator approval, a Zoom administrator may need to approve the app before it can be used.

4. Permissions and Data Access

The exact Zoom permissions requested are shown during the Zoom authorization flow. Agni only requests permissions needed to provide the configured app functionality.

Depending on the app version and enabled features, Agni may access some or all of the following categories of Zoom data:

Data category Examples Purpose
Zoom user/account information User ID, account ID, name, email address Connect the correct Zoom user or account to Agni
Meeting metadata Meeting ID, topic, host, timestamps, meeting link Create, display, sync, or associate meetings with Agni workflows
Calendar or scheduling data Meeting time, duration, related scheduling information Coordinate appointment or meeting workflows
Meeting content, where enabled Recordings, transcripts, summaries, participant-related records Provide configured call intelligence or workflow features
Authorization data Access tokens, refresh tokens, installation status, revocation status Maintain or disconnect the integration securely

Agni does not access Zoom data that is not authorized by the permissions granted to the app.

5. How Agni Uses Zoom Data

Agni may use Zoom data to:

  • connect a Zoom account to an Agni workspace;
  • enable Zoom-related workflows inside Agni;
  • sync or display meeting links and meeting metadata;
  • connect meetings or scheduled events to contacts, campaigns, appointments, or CRM records;
  • generate configured summaries, transcripts, or call intelligence outputs where enabled;
  • provide support, troubleshooting, security, and audit functions;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Agni does not sell Zoom data. Agni does not use Zoom data to train AI models for other customers unless expressly authorized in writing.

6. Using the Zoom App

After installation:

  1. Open Agni.
  2. Navigate to the workflow where Zoom is used, such as integrations, appointments, meetings, contacts, campaigns, or call/session workflows.
  3. Choose the relevant Zoom-connected account or meeting option.
  4. Configure the workflow in Agni.
  5. Save the configuration.
  6. Test the workflow before using it with customers, leads, call participants, or meeting participants.

The available actions may depend on your Agni plan, organization settings, Zoom permissions, and the features enabled for your workspace.

7. Disconnecting or Removing the Zoom App

You can disconnect Zoom from Agni by:

  1. Signing in to Agni.
  2. Going to Integrations or the Zoom connection settings.
  3. Selecting the connected Zoom account.
  4. Choosing Disconnect, Remove, or the equivalent action.

You can also revoke the app from Zoom:

  1. Sign in to your Zoom account.
  2. Open Zoom App Marketplace or your account's installed apps settings.
  3. Find the Agni/ravan.ai app.
  4. Choose Remove, Uninstall, or Revoke.

After removal, Agni will stop accessing new Zoom data through the revoked authorization. Previously processed data may remain in Agni according to customer settings, applicable agreements, the Privacy Policy, and backup retention practices.

8. Requesting Data Deletion

To request deletion of Zoom-related data processed by Agni, contact:

privacy@ravan.ai

Please include:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your organization or workspace name;
  • the Zoom account email involved;
  • the type of data you want deleted;
  • any relevant meeting IDs, timestamps, contact names, or workflow references.

If the data is controlled by a business customer, we may direct the request to that customer or work with that customer to respond.

9. Troubleshooting

The app will not install

Check that:

  • you are signed in to the correct Zoom account;
  • your organization allows app installations;
  • you have administrator approval if required;
  • you are signed in to the correct Agni workspace.

The integration disconnected

Try reconnecting Zoom from the Agni integrations page. If the issue continues, contact support with the time of the issue and any error message shown.

The wrong Zoom account is connected

Disconnect the current Zoom account, sign in to the correct Zoom account, and reconnect through Agni.

Meeting data is missing

Confirm that:

  • the relevant Zoom permissions were granted;
  • the meeting belongs to the connected Zoom account;
  • the feature is enabled in Agni;
  • your Agni workspace has access to the relevant workflow.
